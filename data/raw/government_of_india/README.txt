Place Government of India central ministry/department PDFs in this directory.

Source category: Government of India

APPROVED central government ministry portals ONLY:
- socialjustice.gov.in  (Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment)
- tribal.gov.in          (Ministry of Tribal Affairs)
- minorityaffairs.gov.in (Ministry of Minority Affairs)
- education.gov.in       (Ministry of Education)
- dohe-education.gov.in  (Department of Higher Education)
- Other CENTRAL government ministry .gov.in portals

NOT permitted even if .gov.in:
- State government portals (e.g. oasis.gov.in, scholarship.up.gov.in)
- myScheme.gov.in (aggregator)
- NIC portals hosting state scheme PDFs
- University/college .ac.in domains

Naming convention: goi_<ministry_short>_<scheme_name>_<academic_year>.pdf

Example: goi_mSJE_postmatric_sc_2024_25.pdf
